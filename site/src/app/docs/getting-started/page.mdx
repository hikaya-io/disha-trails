# Getting started

This page takes you from the beta invite to a map that works with no signal.

## Join the beta

Disha is in beta on TestFlight, Apple's app for testing apps before release. It needs an iPhone with iOS 17.5 or later.

[Join the waitlist](/#waitlist) and we'll email an invite when seats open. Then:

1. Install TestFlight from the App Store if you don't have it.
2. Open the invite email on your iPhone and accept it in TestFlight.
3. Install Disha from TestFlight.

## Download an area before you leave

Do this on Wi-Fi.

1. Open the **Offline** tab and choose **New area**. Or, on the map, choose **New area from the screen** to download what you are looking at.
2. Check the size estimate.
3. Start the download and wait for it to finish.

See [Offline maps](/docs/offline-maps/) for what an area includes.

## Check it works offline

Turn on airplane mode, then open the map over the area you downloaded. Pan and zoom, and tap a trail to see its elevation profile. If the map is blank, check the **Offline** tab: the download may not have finished.

## Location

Disha asks for your location only when you tap the locate button. It uses it to show where you are on the map, and your location stays on your phone.
