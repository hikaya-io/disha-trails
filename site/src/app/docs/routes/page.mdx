# Routes

The **Routes** tab holds routes you bring or draw. Routes are stored on your phone and leave it only when you share one.

## Import a GPX file

Open the **Routes** tab and choose **Import GPX**. You can also share a `.gpx` file to Disha from Files or Mail.

## Look at a route

Tap a route to see its elevation profile. Drag the A and B markers to see the distance and climb of just that section. Routes you add show on the map in their own colors.

## Draw a new route

1. Open the **Routes** tab and choose **Create new route**.
2. Drag the B marker along a trail. The route follows the paths in the map you have installed, with live distance, climb and descent.
3. Use **Undo** to step back. Hold still for a moment to turn around.
4. Choose **Save**.

Download the area first. The route follows the paths in your offline map.

<Callout type="tip">
A dashed stretch doesn't follow a known path. Check it on the ground.
</Callout>
