# FAQ

## Using Disha

### Does Disha need a signal?

No, once you have downloaded an area. Download on Wi-Fi before you go. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/).

### Why is my map blank in airplane mode?

The area probably didn't finish downloading, or you are looking at a place you didn't download. Disha shows a low-detail world map everywhere and full detail only inside your areas. Check the **Offline** tab.

### Can I use my own route?

Yes. Import a GPX file, or draw a route on the map. See [Routes](/docs/routes/).

### Can I ask for a trail?

Yes. [Request a trail](/docs/requests/), or add a 👍 to its request if it has one. Votes decide what is built next.

## Your data

### Do I need an account?

No. The beta has no accounts.

### Does Disha track me?

No. Disha asks for your location only when you tap the locate button, and your location stays on your phone. Routes you import or draw are stored on your phone, and they leave it only when you share one.

### Where does the map data come from?

The map is built from OpenStreetMap data, with terrain from Copernicus. See the [map credits](/privacy/#map-credits).

## The app

### What does Disha cost?

Disha will be a paid app. Pricing is not final.

### Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Disha is iPhone only for now.
