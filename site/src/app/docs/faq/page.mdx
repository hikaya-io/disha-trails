# FAQ

## Using Disha

### Does Disha need a signal?

No, once you have downloaded an area. Download on Wi-Fi before you go. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/).

### Why is my map blank in airplane mode?

Either the area didn't finish downloading or you're outside it. Outside your areas Disha shows only a low-detail world map. Check the **Offline** tab.

### Can I use my own route?

Yes. Import a GPX file or draw a route on the map. See [Routes](/docs/routes/).

### Can I ask for a trail?

Yes. [Request a trail](/docs/requests/) or add a 👍 to its request if it has one. Votes decide what is built next.

## Your data

### Do I need an account?

No. You can use the maps, guides, and routes without an account.

### Does Disha track me?

No. Disha asks for your location only when you tap the locate button and your location stays on your phone. Routes you import or draw are stored on your phone and leave it only when you share one.

### Where does the map data come from?

The map is built from OpenStreetMap data with terrain from Copernicus. See the [map credits](/privacy/#map-credits).

## The app

### Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Disha is iPhone only for now.
