# Open trail data

Disha's guides are built on open data and hikers keep them current with reports from the trail.

## What it covers

- Seasonal conditions, such as snow, closures, and washed-out sections.
- Water points, including whether a source is flowing.
- Stops such as huts, campsites, shops, and viewpoints.

## Keeping it current

Hikers vote reports up or down so current reports rise above old ones. At the end of each season, older comments are rolled into a short summary you can compare year to year.

Stops and the base map come from OpenStreetMap and reports come from hikers. Moderators review what hikers add. Verified points go back into OpenStreetMap over time so every map that uses it gets them.

Reports are coming to the trail pages on this site too, so anyone can read them without the app.
