# Offline maps

Disha keeps map data on your phone in areas you download. Once an area is downloaded, you can use it with no connection.

## What an area includes

An area has several layers.

- The basemap, with paths, water and land cover from OpenStreetMap.
- Waymarked hiking routes.
- Contour lines and terrain shading.
- Points of interest.

Disha also includes a low-detail map of the world. It works everywhere, but you need an area for detail.

## Download an area

1. Open the **Offline** tab and choose **New area**, or choose **New area from the screen** on the map.
2. Review the size estimate.
3. Start the download. Progress shows while it runs.

You can cancel a download, and you can delete an area when you no longer need it. After a download, Disha shows the size of each layer.

## Where maps are available

[Where maps are available](/docs/offline-maps/coverage/) explains the maps page and its tags.

## Limits in the beta

<Callout type="beta">
Disha doesn't notify you when a download finishes, so check the **Offline** tab before you leave. Removing a guide keeps its offline map, so delete the area under **Offline** if you want the space back.
</Callout>
