# Offline maps

Disha stores maps on your phone as areas you download. A downloaded area works with no connection.

## What an area includes

- The basemap with paths, water, and land cover from OpenStreetMap.
- Waymarked hiking routes.
- Contour lines and terrain shading.
- Points of interest.

Disha also has a low-detail world map that works everywhere. For full detail, download an area.

## Download an area

1. Open the **Offline** tab and choose **New area** or choose **New area from the screen** on the map.
2. Review the size estimate.
3. Start the download.

You can cancel a download or delete an area later. After a download Disha shows the size of each layer.

## Where maps are available

[Where maps are available](/docs/offline-maps/coverage/) explains the maps page and its tags.

## Limits in the beta

<Callout type="beta">
  Disha doesn't notify you when a download finishes so check the **Offline** tab before you leave.
  Removing a guide keeps its offline map so delete the area under **Offline** if you want the space
  back.
</Callout>
