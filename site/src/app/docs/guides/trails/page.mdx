# Which trails have guides

The [trails page](/trails/) lists every guide, from those in the app to those people have asked for. It opens on available and coming-soon guides. Use the status filters to see the rest.

## Guide status

<StatusTable kind="guide" />

A guide always comes with its offline map. A trail can also have a map without a guide. The map has the paths, contours and hillshade. A guide adds the water, huts and campsites along the route, with the distance to each.

Trails with only a map are on the [maps page](/maps/). [Where maps are available](/docs/offline-maps/coverage/#map-status) explains the map tags.

## On a trail's page

A trail's page shows its length, download size, map and guide status, and its route on OpenStreetMap. The main button shows what you can do next: request a missing guide or map, or suggest an edit once the guide is published.

## Missing a trail?

[Request a trail](/docs/requests/). Votes decide which guides are made next.
