# Which trails have guides

The [trails page](/trails/) lists every trail guide, from the ones in the app to the ones people have asked for. It opens on the guides you can install and the ones coming soon; the status filters bring in the rest.

## Guide status

<StatusTable kind="guide" />

A guide always comes with its offline map. A trail can have a map without a guide: the map has the paths, contours and hillshade, and a guide adds the water, huts and campsites along the route, with the distance to each. Trails with only a map are on the [maps page](/maps/), and [Where maps are available](/docs/offline-maps/coverage/#map-status) explains the map tags.

## On a trail's page

A trail's page shows its length, download size, map and guide status, and its route on OpenStreetMap. Its main button is what you can do next: request the guide or map it's missing, or suggest an edit once its guide is published.

## Missing a trail?

[Request a trail](/docs/requests/). Votes decide which guides are made next.
