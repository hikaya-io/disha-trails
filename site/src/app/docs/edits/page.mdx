# Suggest an edit

Trails change: springs dry up, huts close, routes move. Once a trail's guide is published and its map is built, its page has a **Suggest an edit** button. It opens a short form on GitHub, so you need a free GitHub account.

## What to report

- A distance or climb that's wrong.
- A stop to add, such as water, a hut, a campsite, a shop or a viewpoint.
- A stop to update or remove, such as a dry spring or a closed hut.
- A route change: a reroute, a closure or a washed-out section.
- A new alternate or variant.
- Conditions such as snow or a hazard.

Say where it is (a stop's name, a distance along the trail or coordinates) and when you saw it. A photo, a GPX file or a park notice helps.

## Confirm other edits

**Open edits** on a trail's page lists the edits already reported. If you've seen the same thing, add a 👍 instead of opening a new one; the most-confirmed edits are made first.

## Fix OpenStreetMap directly

Guides start from OpenStreetMap. If a stop or path is wrong there, you can fix it at [openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org), and every map that uses it gets the fix.
